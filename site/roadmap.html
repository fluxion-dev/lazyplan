Now · shipped

Capture and resume.

Shipped 2026-11-06. Plan apply, recipes, the context packet, and the Focus lens are in the product.

R1Shipped

Plan compiler

Create the plan, milestones, tasks, and criteria in one atomic write. Dry-run, stable keys, idempotent retry. The interview stays; the 30 calls go.

R2Shipped

Plan recipes

Feature, bugfix, and refactor skeletons on top of apply. QA, docs, and refactor tasks are present by default; the words stay empty for the interview.

R3Shipped

Context pack

One budgeted read for a cold session: active plan, doing task, next work, decisions, commits, claims, and unresolved comments. A CLI read, an MCP resource, and hook context.

R4Shipped

Focus lens

See what is in flight: doing tasks, agent claims, blocked work, attention rows, and next candidates. A cockpit for the human supervising the fleet.

Next · shipped

The trust layer.

Shipped 2026-11-06. Approvals, evidence, questions, and the review packet are in the product.

R5Shipped

Approval snapshots and plan diff

Approve a revision, then see exactly what changed and why. Drift becomes visible without blocking the agent.

R6Shipped

Evidence ledger

Attach the command, exit code, commit, and artifact to a criterion. lazyplan records the proof; the agent or CI runs the check.

R7Shipped

Review packet

One Markdown handoff with the plan, diff, criteria coverage, commits, PRs, lint, audit, and open questions. For a human or a reviewer agent.

R8Shipped

Open questions and plan-ready gate

Keep the ambiguity hunt. A plan cannot claim readiness while material questions are open.

Later · shipped

Parallel agents and handoff.

Shipped 2026-11-06. Sessions, touch-point-aware claims, external references, and the ship note are in the product.

R9Shipped

Session records

A session has a start, an end, a summary, and handoff notes. The feed can be read per session, so the next agent knows why the last one chose what it did.

R10Shipped

Parallel-safe claims

Tasks carry touch-points. Next avoids overlapping claims, the worktree is recorded, and finish warns when commits overlap another open task.

R11Shipped

Outcome digest

Close a plan into a ship note for a PR body or changelog: why, what shipped, evidence, residual risks, and follow-ups. No issue mirroring.

R12Shipped

External reference links

Point a plan at an epic or issue without copying it. A read-only link preserves the connection; lazyplan never writes back.

Not on the roadmap

We are not building a tracker.

lazyplan is for the thinking before commitment. Committed work belongs in your tracker, and we will not mirror it there.

  • No two-way Jira, Linear, or GitHub Issues sync.
  • No plans mirrored as issues.
  • No sprints, estimates, assignees, or date planning.
  • No built-in LLM or cloud account.
  • No agent runtime that creates branches or commits.
  • No real-time multiplayer editing.

Follow the work.

The roadmap is a living document. The repo keeps the reasoning; lazyplan keeps the execution.
